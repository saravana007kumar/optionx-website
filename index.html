Investor Charter — Research Analyst

As per SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/81 dated 02 June 2025

A. Vision & Mission

Vision: Invest with knowledge & safety.

Mission: Every investor should be able to invest in right investment products based on their needs, manage and monitor them to meet their goals, access reports, and enjoy financial wellness.

B. Details of Business Transacted by the Research Analyst

To publish research reports based on the research activities of the RA. To provide independent, unbiased views on securities. To offer unbiased recommendations, disclosing the financial interests in recommended securities. To provide research recommendations based on analysis of publicly available information and known observations. To conduct an audit annually. To ensure all advertisements adhere to the Advertisement Code for Research Analysts. To maintain records of interactions with all clients including prospective clients. To treat each client with honesty and fairness in all dealings and transactions. To safeguard client data privacy.

C. Details of Services Provided to Investors

Onboarding of clients (KYC & agreement execution). Providing research recommendations / reports. Addressing client queries and grievances.

D. Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Level 1: Lodge complaint directly with the Research Analyst — email: saravanakumar1840@gmail.com | phone: +91 8072005130. The RA shall strive to redress the grievance immediately, but not later than 21 days of receipt.

Level 2: If not resolved, lodge complaint on SCORES 2.0 (SEBI Complaints Redress System) at scores.sebi.gov.in or with RAASB (BSE Ltd).

Level 3: If still unsatisfied, file on SMARTODR platform for online conciliation or arbitration.

SEBI Office: Office of Investor Assistance and Education, SEBI Bhavan, Plot No. C4-A, G Block, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai - 400051. Toll Free: 1800 266 7575.

E. Rights of Investors

Right to receive unbiased research. Right to timely communication of recommendations. Right to transparent disclosure of conflicts of interest. Right to access grievance redressal mechanism. Right to receive clear guidance when dealing in complex and high-risk financial products. Right to exit from financial product/service as per agreed terms. Right to provide feedback. Right against coercive, unfair, and one-sided clauses.

F. Responsibilities of Investors (Do’s and Don’ts)

Do’s: Always deal with SEBI registered Research Analysts. Ensure the RA has a valid registration certificate (check at SEBI website). Read all related documents carefully. Assess your risk appetite before investing. Report any irregularities to SEBI.

Don’ts: Do not provide funds for investment to the Research Analyst. Do not fall prey to luring advertisements or market rumors. Do not get attracted to limited period discounts or other incentive/gifts offered by RA. Do not share login credentials or trading passwords with anyone.
