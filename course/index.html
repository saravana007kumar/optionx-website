The Program

Understand how the market actually behaves

You will study broad areas such as Footprint Charts, straddle behaviour, India VIX, Vega, implied volatility and the basics of option selling, then connect those ideas through practical market analysis and risk thinking.

This is education. It is not a tips service, not a research-call subscription, and not a promise of profits.

What You'll Learn

Broad areas covered in the program

These are the themes the educational program is built around. They are not a fixed syllabus, module list, or promised set of deliverables.

Footprint Chart

How to read participation and order-flow context at price, instead of looking at candles alone.

Straddle Behaviour

How combined option premium tends to behave as the market expands, contracts, or stays range-bound.

India VIX

Using India VIX as a lens on market uncertainty and the broader volatility environment.

Vega & Implied Volatility

How option prices can respond when implied volatility changes, and why that matters in decision-making.

Basics of Option Selling

Foundational ideas behind option selling, including risk awareness. Not a list of ready-made trades.

Practical Market Analysis

Connecting the above ideas to live market context, so learning stays grounded in how markets move.

Risk Management Concepts

A disciplined way to think about uncertainty, exposure and capital protection while studying the market.
